# Cinematic Viewing — Website Brief & Ideas

**Domain:** [CinematicViewing.com](https://cinematicviewing.com)  
**Audience:** Luxury chalet & apartment owners, agencies, and brokers in Switzerland (rental + sales).  
**Goal:** Impress a decision-maker and win bookings for premium property films.

**Reference work:** FPV one-shot cinematic house tour (Normandie) — 4K, ~80s, continuous flow through interior/exterior. Style to lead with on the site.

---

## 1. Positioning (what you are selling)

**One line:**  
*Cinematic films that make Swiss luxury properties feel as exceptional as they are — before a client ever visits.*

**Elevator pitch (30 seconds):**  
*I’m a filmmaker with 30+ years of experience and a licensed drone operator. I create immersive property films for high-end chalets and apartments: smooth gimbal tours, breathtaking aerials, and — where it suits the property — single-take FPV fly-throughs indoors and out. I can also turn your existing photo library into cinematic moving films using top-class AI and cinema-level prompting — so every listing looks premium across web, social, and presentations.*

**Differentiators to repeat everywhere:**
- 30+ years in filmmaking (not “a drone guy with a camera”)
- Licensed drone operation (Swiss compliance matters to agencies)
- **Three production modes:** gimbal / aerial / FPV (indoor + outdoor where safe & permitted)
- **Photo to film:** Top-class AI with cinema-level prompting (30+ years of filmmaking judgment — not generic automation)
- One-shot FPV tours as a *signature* product (rare, memorable, shareable)

## 3. Features to build on the website

### Must-have
- **Full-screen hero video** (autoplay muted, your Normandie-style tour or a 15s cut)
- **Service cards** with short video loops per mode (FPV / Gimbal / Aerial / Photo-to-cinema)
- **Showreel grid** with category filters
- **“Deliverables” strip:** 4K master, vertical cuts for Instagram/Reels, 30–90s web hero, still grabs
- **Trust bar:** years experience · drone license · Switzerland · insured (if true) · discretion on site
- **Contact CTA** sticky on mobile: *Book a viewing call* or *Request a quote*

### Impress-the-client extras
- **Before/after:** static listing photos vs your film (split screen)
- **Sample deliverables PDF** download: “What you receive after a shoot”
- **Region tags:** Verbier, Gstaad, Zermatt, Geneva, etc. (even if portfolio is growing — signals you know the market)
- **Private preview links** (password page per property — agencies love this)
- **Languages:** English + French (minimum for Swiss luxury); German optional
- **Accessibility:** large type, high contrast (you already care about readability — mirror that on site)

### Nice-to-have later
- Client login / asset library
- Pricing “from CHF …” or packages (see section 6)
- Blog: “Why FPV tours convert luxury rentals” (SEO + authority)

### Services (four blocks)

#### 1. Cinematic property films (gimbal)
**Say:**  
*Smooth, deliberate camera movement through every room — architecture, light, and views presented like a feature film. Ideal for sales brochures, agency websites, and hero banners.*

**Bullet deliverables:** 4K film · room-by-room pacing · twilight exteriors optional · music licensing included (if you offer it)

#### 2. Aerial & landscape (licensed drone)
**Say:**  
*Reveal the setting: valley, lake, ski access, privacy, and scale. Fully licensed drone work for compliant, stunning exteriors.*

**Note for Swiss client:** Mention respect for local drone rules and coordination with property managers — builds trust.

#### 3. FPV indoor & outdoor tours
**Say:**  
*A single flowing flight path through the property — from driveway to living room to terrace — in one immersive take. The format luxury buyers remember and share.*

**Say honestly:** Indoor FPV only where safe, permitted, and appropriate; not every chalet needs it — you advise on the best format.

**Tie to example:** Feature the Normandie-style tour prominently: *“See what a one-shot tour feels like.”*

#### 4. Photo to film (AI + cinema-level prompting)
**Say:**  
*Already have beautiful photography? I transform your existing image library into cinematic moving films — using top-class AI, guided by 30+ years in filmmaking and cinema-level prompting, so every sequence feels directed and premium, not generic.*

**Sub-benefit:** Fast turnaround, consistent brand look across many properties — ideal when a full reshoot is not possible.

### Process (simple 4 steps)

1. **Consultation** — Property, goals (sale vs rental), brand style, timeline  
2. **Creative plan** — Gimbal, aerial, FPV, or photo-to-film — or a combination  
3. **Shoot day** — Efficient, minimal disruption; golden hour when possible  
4. **Delivery** — Graded 4K master + cut-downs for web and social

## 7. Visual & brand direction (luxury, Swiss, cinematic)

- **Palette:** Deep charcoal, warm white, single accent (champagne gold or alpine blue)  
- **Typography:** Elegant serif for headlines (e.g. Cormorant, Playfair) + clean sans for body (e.g. Inter, DM Sans)  
- **Motion:** Slow fades, no bouncy UI — feel like a film studio, not a template  
- **Imagery:** Full-bleed video; minimal stock photos  
- **Logo wordmark:** *Cinematic Viewing* — tagline underneath: *Luxury Property Films*

---

## 8. What will impress your Swiss contact specifically

1. **Lead with their world** — chalets, apartments, Verbier/Gstaad language, rental *and* sale  
2. **Show the FPV example early** — it’s your “wow” differentiator  
3. **Photo-to-film** — solves “we already have photos” objection  
4. **Licensed & professional** — reduces agency risk  
5. **Deliverables list** — they buy clarity, not just “a video”  
6. **Discretion** — many luxury owners want privacy; say you work quietly  
7. **French/English** — even a bilingual toggle signals seriousness in Switzerland
